# DarkRelay™

Free, local-first software for Windows and Android.
No telemetry. No accounts. No cloud. Just useful buttons.

*local-first • low-drama • useful buttons*

---

## Programs, for Windows

### Lazarus PDF
A free, local PDF reader and editor for Windows. Mark up, fill in, edit text, redact, OCR scans, merge and split pages. Your original is never overwritten.
AGPL-3.0 · no telemetry

[Download](https://github.com/Crazy8697/LazarusPDF/releases/latest) · [Source](https://github.com/Crazy8697/LazarusPDF)

### Playlist Flow
Orders your Spotify playlists by musical key and tempo so each song leads smoothly into the next, then saves the new order back. Works with Spotify (Premium needed).
Apache-2.0 · no telemetry

[Download](https://github.com/Crazy8697/PlaylistFlow/releases/latest) · [Source](https://github.com/Crazy8697/PlaylistFlow)

### PhoneDeck
Run your Android phone's apps in their own windows on your PC, with your keyboard and mouse. Built on scrcpy, over USB or wireless debugging.
Apache-2.0 · no telemetry

[Download](https://github.com/Crazy8697/PhoneDeck/releases/latest) · [Source](https://github.com/Crazy8697/PhoneDeck)

## Apps, for Android

### MicroDeck
Turns your Android phone into a magnifier for soldering and fine work. Lens picker, zoom, manual focus, torch, and a grid overlay. Pairs with PhoneDeck for a big view on your monitor.
MIT · no telemetry · Android 13+

[Download](https://github.com/Crazy8697/MicroDeck/releases/latest) · [Source](https://github.com/Crazy8697/MicroDeck)

### Dead Drop Finance
Envelope budgeting that stays yours. Safe to Spend, bill reminders, CSV import, all encrypted on your own device. Android app, Windows program, and an optional sync server you run yourself.
Apache-2.0 · no telemetry · not financial advice

[Download](https://github.com/Crazy8697/DeadDropFinance/releases/latest) · [Source](https://github.com/Crazy8697/DeadDropFinance) · [Server](https://github.com/Crazy8697/DeadDropServer)
